Automotive Service & Warranty Compliance Agent (ASWCA) 

The Business Problem: ]

When customers bring their vehicles to an official dealership service center in Europe, the service advisors and mechanics log raw repair notes, diagnostic codes, and customer interaction details into a central ticketing system.

Compliance Challenge:

1. logs frequently contain sensitive Customer PII (names, phone numbers, home addresses) and Vehicle Identifiers (VINs).
2. warranty claims must be cross-referenced against strict Manufacturer Warranty Policies and Technical Service Bulletins (TSBs) to determine
   if a repair is covered under warranty or if it violates compliance guidelines (e.g., unauthorized modifications or user-induced damage).
   

Requirements : 

1. Sanitizes the raw service report (scrubbing PII and recognizing VINs/License plates).
2. Retrieves relevant warranty policies and TSBs from a Qdrant vector database using hybrid search (dense + sparse with RRF) and payload filtering (e.g., filtering by region or tier).
3. Drafts a structured warranty claim assessment (Approved / Denied / Additional Review Required) with justification and remediation steps.
4. Exposes the entire flow via a FastAPI endpoint with state checkpointing.
